# EDA Findings

1. Mutual fund NAVs showed steady growth during 2023 bull market conditions.

2. SBI Mutual Fund maintained highest AUM dominance across 2022–2025.

3. SIP inflows increased consistently and reached all-time highs in late 2025.

4. Younger investors contributed major SIP participation across transactions.

5. Male investors were slightly higher in count compared to female investors.

6. Maharashtra and Karnataka contributed high SIP investment volumes.

7. T30 cities contributed majority of total investments compared to B30 regions.

8. Equity sectors like Banking and IT occupied major portfolio allocation.

9. Most selected mutual funds showed positive correlation in NAV movement.

10. Retail participation in mutual funds increased significantly between 2022 and 2025.
